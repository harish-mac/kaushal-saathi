# PMKVY Overview
Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is the flagship skill development scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, implemented through NSDC. PMKVY 4.0 was approved for FY 2022-23 to 2025-26 with an outlay of Rs 6,000 crore. Check skillindia.gov.in for any extension or successor version.

# PMKVY Components
PMKVY 4.0 has three components: Short Term Training (STT), Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL, also called upskilling), and Special Projects (SP) for marginalised groups and difficult geographies.

# Short Term Training (STT)
STT is classroom plus on-the-job training for people who are new to a trade. Duration is roughly 200 to 600 hours depending on the job role. Training is at NSQF level 5 and below. On-the-job training is mandatory for many job roles. New-age courses include AI, drones, IoT, 3D printing and blockchain.

# Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)
RPL is for people who already work in a trade (for example a tailor, electrician, mason or driver) but have no formal certificate. They are assessed, given a short bridge training of about 30 to 132 hours, and receive a government-recognised certificate. RPL mainly targets people in the informal or unregulated sector.

# Age Eligibility
Short Term Training and Special Projects: candidate age 15 to 45 years. RPL: age 18 to 59 years. Note: some third-party websites quote 17-45 or 18-45; the NSDC FAQ says 15-45 for STT. Always confirm with the training centre.

# Documents Required
Aadhaar (used for e-KYC on Skill India Digital Hub), a bank account in the candidate's name, a working mobile number, and basic education and prior experience details. Keep a passport-size photo ready. A training centre may ask for extra documents.

# How to Apply
1. Register and complete e-KYC on the Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH) portal or app using Aadhaar.
2. Select PMKVY and choose a course or job role.
3. Pick a nearby training centre and batch, checking batch start date, centre name and timing.
4. Show interest in the batch. The training centre reviews the request and enrols you.
5. Attend training, take the assessment, and receive your certificate.

# Cost and Fraud Warning
Training under PMKVY is government funded and free for the candidate. Never pay any person or centre for registration, a seat or a certificate. Report fraud in the name of PMKVY to the official grievance helpdesk.

# Certificate and Placement
Successful candidates are assessed by a third-party assessor and receive a certificate aligned to the National Skills Qualification Framework. Under PMKVY 4.0 the focus is on helping trained candidates choose their own career path, with placement support through training partners rather than guaranteed jobs.
