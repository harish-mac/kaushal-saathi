# PMKVY in Very Simple Words
PMKVY is a government scheme that teaches you a job skill for free and gives you an official certificate. The certificate helps you get a job or start your own work. You do not pay anything for the training.

# Simple Meaning of Difficult Words
- Skill training: learning how to do a job properly, for example tailoring, electrician work, computer work or driving.
- Certificate: a paper from the government that proves you know the skill.
- STT (Short Term Training): a course for people who are starting fresh, with no work experience in that trade.
- RPL (Recognition of Prior Learning): for people who already do the work but have no paper. They get a short test and some training, and then a certificate.
- Aadhaar e-KYC: confirming who you are using your Aadhaar and the OTP on your phone.
- Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH): the official website and app where you register and choose a course.
- Training centre: the place near you where the classes happen.
- Batch: one group of students that starts a course on a fixed date.
- NSQF: the government's level system for skills. You do not need to worry about it.

# Step by Step: How to Register (Very Simple)
Step 1. Keep your Aadhaar card and your phone with the Aadhaar-linked number ready.
Step 2. Open the Skill India Digital Hub website or app on your phone.
Step 3. Sign up and confirm your identity with Aadhaar and the OTP that comes to your phone.
Step 4. Fill in your name, age, education and any work experience.
Step 5. Choose PMKVY and pick the course or job you want to learn.
Step 6. Pick a training centre near your home and look at the batch start date and timing.
Step 7. Press the button to show interest in that batch.
Step 8. The training centre will check your request and add you to the batch. They may call you.
Step 9. Go to the classes, give the final test, and collect your certificate.

# If You Cannot Use the Internet
Go to your nearest PMKVY training centre or a Common Service Centre (CSC) and ask them to help you register. Take your Aadhaar card, bank passbook and phone. Remember that you should not pay any money for this.

# Where to Find Official Information
- Skill India main website: skillindia.gov.in (say it as: skill india dot gov dot in)
- PMKVY official website: pmkvyofficial.org (say it as: P M K V Y official dot org)
- Skill India Digital Hub: skillindiadigital.gov.in (say it as: skill india digital dot gov dot in)

# Safety Tips
- Training under PMKVY is free. If anyone asks for money for a seat, a form or a certificate, say no. It is probably a fraud.
- Never tell anyone your OTP, bank card number or PIN. Not even someone who says they are from the government.
- Only use the official websites listed here.

# Common Questions in Simple Words
Q: Do I need to pay? A: No. The training is free.
Q: I did not study much. Can I join? A: Many courses are open to people with low education. The training centre will tell you which courses fit you.
Q: I already work as a tailor, electrician or mason. What do I do? A: Ask for RPL. It gives you a certificate for the skill you already have.
Q: Will I get a job? A: The certificate helps and the training partners try to help with jobs, but a job is not guaranteed.
Q: Which documents are needed? A: Aadhaar, a bank account in your name, your mobile number, and your education details. Keep a photo ready too.
